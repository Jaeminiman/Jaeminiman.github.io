About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Construction Innovation Laboratory at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Seokho Chi, and currently a visiting Ph.D. student at the University of Michigan with Prof. Sanghyun Lee. My research builds timely and reliable digital representations of construction environments — combining neural 3D reconstruction, SLAM, and vision–language models so that dynamic job sites can be monitored, understood, and managed from data rather than from manual inspection.

Research Interests

  • Timely and reliable digital representation of construction environments
  • Automation in construction through computer vision and robotics
  • Digital twin-driven construction management and human–robot collaboration
